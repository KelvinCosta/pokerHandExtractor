---

### Requesting a new platform
If your main platform is not supported yet, kindly open an **Issue** [here](https://github.com/KelvinCosta/pokerHandExtractor/issues/new) on GitHub and **attach sample hand history files** from that room. This helps us immensely in reverse-engineering the format to add it to the extractor!
